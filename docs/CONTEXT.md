## Subfolders
- **`CODEMAPS`**: Visual and textual code maps of specific subsystems for rapid navigation and dependency tracing.
- **`adr`**: Architecture Decision Records detailing key technical design decisions made throughout the life of the project.
- **`audits`**: Specific diagnostic audit logs and assessments compiled for security, performance, or capability analysis.
- **`decisions`**: Detailed studies and comparative assessments of technologies, protocols, or routing options under consideration.
- **`monitoring`**: Configurations, dashboards, and instructions for observability, metrics collection, and alerting setups.
- **`plans`**: Phased engineering and remediation roadmaps describing exact development steps for features or fixes.
- **`research`**: Scientific and empirical studies on model adaptabilities, vertical alignments, or specialized methodology behaviors.
- **`security`**: Analyses of security posture, encryption requirements, SAIF compliance checklists, and risk remediation guides.
