# blackbox.ai

Saved 2026-08-28. Values measured from computed styles.
**The most relevant of the four references, and the least safe to copy.**

## What is actually there
- Ground `#050505`; text `#F2F2F0` warm off-white; muted `#666664` / `#585857`;
  hairlines as `rgba(255,255,255,0.12)`. Accent orange `#FF6901` on 22 elements.
- Type: **Berkeley Mono on 1142 elements** — mono is the primary typeface of the entire
  site, not an accent. Supported by `darkerGrotesque` and `archivo` in small roles.
- Headings 44px / 40px, weight 500, tracking -0.66px. Flat hierarchy: nearly every
  section header is the same size. Rhythm comes from spacing, not from size contrast.
- Nav items carry **inline evidence badges**: `PROMPTZ / PII STRIPPED`,
  `TOK/S / VERIFIED` with the claim and its source attached to the label itself.

## Why this one matters most
Its copy is doing exactly what Reasoner's product does: *"Independent measurements, not
claims."* / *"Check it yourself."* / a speed claim attributed to a named third-party
benchmark with a date. It attaches provenance to assertions at the navigation level.
That is Reasoner's VERIFIED / HYPOTHESIS / UNKNOWN thesis, already shipped by someone
else, as an information-design pattern rather than as decoration.

## TAKE
- **Provenance attached to the claim, inline.** Every number carries its source and date
  in the same visual unit. This is the single most transferable idea in all four refs.
- Flat type hierarchy where spacing, not size, creates rhythm. Holds up far better on a
  dense application surface than a dramatic size ladder does.
- Mono as the primary voice for a product about measurement.

## DO NOT TAKE
- `#050505` + `#FF6901`. Banlist: "Near-black background with a single acid-green or
  vermilion accent." This is that entry, exactly.
- Berkeley Mono itself — commercial licence, paid per-seat. If mono goes primary, budget
  for it or pick a libre alternative.

## Banlist status
HIT on palette. Retained for **provenance-inline pattern and flat hierarchy**.
