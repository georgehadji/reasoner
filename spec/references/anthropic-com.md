# anthropic.com

Saved 2026-08-28. Observed directly.

## What is actually there
- Ground: warm cream/ivory, near-black text. This is the palette already cloned into
  `ui-next/src/app/globals.css` (`--bg: #FAF9F5`, `--accent: #96401F` "Anthropic coral").
- Display: a geometric sans with a distinctive slashed-A wordmark. Not a serif.
- Hero is **asymmetric and left-aligned**: large headline occupying the left ~55%, small
  body paragraph set right and vertically offset. Not a centred stack.
- Emphasis device: underline on two words inside the headline ("research", "products").
- Nav: logo-left / links-centre / dark CTA pill right.
- Below the fold: full-bleed near-black panel carrying a collage of small images.

## TAKE
- The asymmetric hero split. Big left headline + small right body is a genuinely better
  structure than the centred stack, and it survives on a dense app surface.
- Underline-as-emphasis inside a headline. Cheap, typographic, no decoration required.
- The abrupt light→dark full-bleed panel transition as a section device.

## DO NOT TAKE
- The palette. Any of it. This is the clone source; `spec/banlist.md` names it verbatim.
- The typeface pairing.
- The nav arrangement — banlist: "Nav as logo-left / three-links-centre / sign-in-right."

## Banlist status
HIT on palette, HIT on nav. This reference is retained for **layout proportion only**.
