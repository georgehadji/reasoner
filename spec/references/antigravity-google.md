# antigravity.google

Saved 2026-08-28. Values measured from computed styles, not remembered.

## What is actually there
- Ground `#121317` near-black; dominant surface grey `#45474D`; white text; secondary
  `#5F6368`; panel `#202124`. Cool neutral throughout — no warm accent anywhere.
- Blue `#1A73E8` appears on only 3 elements. The palette is effectively achromatic.
- Type: **Google Sans Flex** (variable) at weight **450** — a non-standard weight axis
  position, not 400 and not 500. One family doing display, body and UI.
- h1 at **80px**, h2 at 42px. Very large jump, no intermediate step.

## TAKE
- **One variable family at an odd weight axis.** 450 reads as deliberate where 400/500
  read as default. Cheap distinctiveness, one font file, no licence problem.
- Achromatic discipline: a near-monochrome system where the single chromatic value is so
  rare it becomes an event. Directly applicable to Reasoner's epistemic tokens — if the
  interface is grey, `--ok`/`--warn`/`--unknown` carry all the colour meaning.
- The 80px→42px jump with nothing between it.

## DO NOT TAKE
- Near-black ground as-is. Banlist bans near-black + single accent; Antigravity only
  escapes because its accent is near-absent. That is a hard trick to land.
- Google Sans Flex itself — not licensable outside Google.

## Banlist status
PARTIAL on near-black ground. Retained for **weight-axis and achromatic strategy**.
