- Redis: required for rate limiting, circuit breaking, webhook dedup, and token cache
- Event bus retries handlers 3x with backoff before dead-lettering
- LLM fallback chain returns DegradedLLMResponse on total failure
- PipelineState fields per method (e.g., socratic_state, debate_state) are dict[str, Any] — never subscripted directly
- Known layer violation: domain/preset_core.py imports from infrastructure.llm.registry
- Known layer violation: api/streaming.py instantiates ReasonerPipeline directly (bypasses CQRS)
